Build a comprehensive, modern, dark-themed "RePitch" ACL Rehabilitation Interactive Web Portal featuring real-time AI posture guidance, guided single-repetition exercise sets, automated clinician reporting, and video telemetry playback.

- Design Aesthetic: High-end athletic-clinical dark mode (Background: `#0A0A0C` / `#0F172A`, Cards: `#1E293B` / `#18181B` with subtle borders `#334155`, Neon Cyan accent: `#00F2FE`, Emerald: `#10B981`, Amber Warning: `#FFB300`, Rose/Red Alert: `#EF4444`).

### 1. 🏃 PATIENT VIEW (Recovery Portal)

#### A. Header & Daily Recovery Readiness
- Patient greeting with streak counter (e.g., "Day 12 • 4-Day Streak 🔥") and earned trophy points (e.g., "350 🏆").
- Recovery Checklist / Daily Check-in card with interactive sliders for Pain (1-10), Knee Swelling level (Mild/Moderate/Severe), and Sleep Quality.
- Roo Mascot Companion: An encouraging kangaroo rehab mascot ("Roo") displaying contextual quips and speech bubbles (e.g., "Let's lock that knee straight today! Zero extension lag!").

#### B. 4-Phase Recovery Roadmap
- Visual milestone subway-style timeline:
  - Phase 1: Acute Recovery & Extension Lock (Active / Current)
  - Phase 2: Neuromuscular Control & Hypertrophy (Locked)
  - Phase 3: Dynamic Loading & Agility (Locked)
  - Phase 4: Return to Sport & Cutting (Locked)

#### C. Phase 1 Exercise Protocol (1 Set × 1 Rep Protocol)
Every exercise in Phase 1 must follow a strict clinical protocol of **1 Set × 1 Repetition**:
1. **Quad Sets with Isometric Lock**: Lie/sit with towel under ankle, contract quadriceps tightly for 5s hold. Joint target: 180° knee extension.
2. **Straight Leg Raise (SLR)**: Lock knee in full extension, elevate leg to 45° without extension lag. Joint target: 135° hip angle.
3. **Heel Slides**: Slowly slide heel towards glutes along a flat surface to achieve controlled bend. Joint target: 110° flexion.
4. **Patellar Mobilizations**: Gentle manual glide of the patella (superior, inferior, medial, lateral). Duration: 60s.

Each exercise card must display:
- Exercise thumbnail / illustration, target joint angle, hold time.
- Clinical warning badge (e.g., "Avoid quad lag", "Keep heel on towel").
- "Start Guided Set" button and "Open AI Camera" button.
